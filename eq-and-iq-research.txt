#Research EQ and IQ

###What is Emotional Intellegence?

Emotional Intelligence (EQ) is the ability to recognize, understand, and manage our own emotions while empathizing with the emotions and perspectives
of others to guide our actions. High or low EQ depends on our level of self-awareness, self-regulation, situational awareness, and empathy.

For example, if my friend 'Billy' makes a cup of tea by pouring milk before the hot water, a low-EQ response would be to immediately criticize him,
imposing a "my way is right" mindset that ignores his background and habits. A high-EQ response involves exercising self-regulation to pause 
the impulse to judge - showing curiosity about his approach and recognizing that there are many valid ways to reach the shared goal of 
making a cup of tea.
